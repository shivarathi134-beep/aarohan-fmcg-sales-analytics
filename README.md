
# Aarohan Consumer Products — FMCG Sales Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics portfolio project focused on understanding sales performance, profitability, product contribution, regional performance, and distribution effectiveness in the FMCG business.

The project uses Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI to explore business data and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem

FMCG businesses need visibility into sales, profitability, product performance, and distribution outcomes to make informed decisions.

This project investigates key business questions using structured data analysis and reporting.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify top- and low-performing products.
- Compare performance across regions.
- Evaluate sales channel and distributor performance.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts, returns, and profitability where supported by the available data.
- Prepare analytical reports and Power BI visualizations.

## Key Business Questions

1. How are sales and profitability performing?
2. Which products contribute most and least to sales and profit?
3. Which regions perform better or worse?
4. How do sales channels and distributors perform?
5. How are discounts and returns related to profitability?

These questions define the scope of the analysis. Findings and recommendations will be reported after the relevant calculations and results have been validated.

## Project Workflow

1. Review and prepare the datasets.
2. Perform supporting analysis in Excel.
3. Query and analyze data using SQL.
4. Explore the data and validate results using Python.
5. Develop a Power BI dashboard.
6. Communicate validated findings through business reports.
